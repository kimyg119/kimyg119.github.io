I am an MS/PhD student at [Robotics and Computer Vision Lab (RCV Lab)](https://www.rcv.sejong.ac.kr/), Sejong University, studying **scalable robot learning** by integrating simulation, real-world data, and synthetic observations into unified training distributions. My research focuses on data-centric robot learning by combining simulation, real-world demonstrations, and generated observations. I treat simulation as a source of structured supervision, and leverage synthetic data to expand observation diversity. The goal is robust manipulation under diverse conditions without relying solely on large-scale real-world data collection.

**MS/PhD**: Department of Intelligent Mechatronics Engineering, Sejong University (2025.03~) | **BS**: School of Intelligent Mechatronics Engineering, Sejong University (2019.03 ~ 2025.02)
